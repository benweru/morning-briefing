    CEO Amjad Masad said ChatGPT is now Replit Agent's default over Anthropic on cost and performance, though Claude models stay selectable; enterprise contracts are now about 15% of Replit's revenue.

    Why it matters: Shows how fast AI coding platforms switch model providers, which matters when he picks models for client work.

    TechCrunch, 17 Oct 2026

    Why it matters: The biggest AI IPO story of the week, and it ties AI gains to the job market he's entering.

    Evos, which builds AI agents that automate work for industrial businesses such as freight brokerages, raised a $9 million pre-seed co-led by Uncork Capital and Field Ventures.

    TechCrunch, 18 Oct 2026

Hiring market

  1. Anthropic's junior technical hiring falls from 27% to 9% of hires (opens in new tab)

    Anthropic's own report says junior employees in technical teams fell from 21% to 14% since early 2025, and hiring managers now weigh autonomy, judgment and creative AI-tool use more than domain expertise.

    Why it matters: Junior roles at AI-heavy firms now reward showing judgment and real AI-tool use, which is worth leading with in his applications.

    TechCrunch, 17 Oct 2026

  2. OpenAI adds 200 Dublin jobs, including forward deployed engineers (opens in new tab)

    OpenAI will add 200 more jobs at its Dublin EU headquarters, three months after announcing 250, with some going to a new Applied AI Lab for forward deployed, solutions and privacy engineers.

    Why it matters: Forward deployed and solutions engineering keep growing as a hiring track that fits his profile.

    Love Dublin, 17 Oct 2026

  3. Disney will hire most of bankrupt Character.AI's staff (opens in new tab)

    A court filing shows Disney agreed to make offers to at least 60 Character.AI employees, an estimated $60 million in yearly pay, after the chatbot startup filed for bankruptcy.

    Why it matters: Big companies are absorbing whole AI teams, a sign that applied-AI experience stays in demand even when startups fail.

    TechCrunch, 18 Oct 2026
