Today in tech

9 stories from 25–26 November 2026, each with a note on why it matters for Ben.

AI and LLMs

  1. JetBrains releases Mellum2.1 Instruct, an open coding model for sub-agents (opens in new tab)

    It is a 12B mixture-of-experts model with 2.5B active parameters, released under Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face with GGUF files and an MTP head that speeds generation up to 1.8x; it generates about half as many tokens as the Thinking variant on agentic coding tasks.

    Why it matters: A small open model he could run locally as a fast worker in coding-agent loops.

    JetBrains Blog, 25 Nov 2026

  2. Anthropic details moving its Messages API from Python to Rust with agents checking parity (opens in new tab)

    Claude wrote a first version of the core request path in about a week; the Rust API uses about 70% less CPU per request and cuts median time to first token by about 20%, with no downtime.

    Why it matters: A concrete case study of using production traffic as a verifier for AI-written code, a pattern he can reuse when migrating live systems.

    Anthropic, 25 Nov 2026

Startups and funding

  1. Nigerian cybersecurity startup Aeon extends pre-seed round to $2 million (opens in new tab)

    The extension follows a $1 million round in September and will fund hiring in engineering and cybersecurity; its products are Aeon Console, an AI-powered compliance platform, and Aeon Edge, a network security device.

    Why it matters: An African AI-security startup that says it is expanding its engineering team.

    TechCabal, 25 Nov 2026

  2. Ten startups, seven of them Kenyan, named finalists for Latitude59 Kenya pitch competition (opens in new tab)

    Chosen from a record 509 applications from 25 countries, the finalists pitch in Nairobi on December 4; the winner gets a fast track to the Latitude59 2027 TOP30 in Tallinn.

    Why it matters: A list of Nairobi-pitching startups, including AI and payments names like Farmsky and ElementPay, worth watching for outreach.

    Disrupt Africa, 26 Nov 2026

Hiring market

  1. SAP reportedly weighs at least 10,000 job cuts in AI-driven restructuring (opens in new tab)

    Bloomberg reported the cuts would affect at least 9% of SAP's global workforce and could change after labour negotiations; Reuters could not verify it and SAP declined to comment.

    Why it matters: Another large software employer reshaping around AI, which keeps pressure on junior engineering hiring.

    Reuters, 26 Nov 2026

  2. Report finds AI-exposed industries hiring fewer beginners rather than laying people off (opens in new tab)

    In the industries using AI most, the share of postings open to candidates with three years of experience or less was 3-8% below the 2021-2026 average for the year ending June 2026.

    Why it matters: Supports leading with shipped projects, since entry-level postings are shrinking where AI use is heaviest.

    Yahoo Finance, 26 Nov 2026

Kenya fintech

  1. Kenyan prosecutors drop criminal case against FlexPay executives in Naivas dispute (opens in new tab)

    The ODPP found the KES 31.2 million ($240,000) dispute civil in nature and told Naivas to seek redress in court, after a five-year partnership that processed more than KES 3 billion in customer transactions.

    Why it matters: A warning on partner-remittance and settlement terms in Kenyan fintech integrations.

    TechCabal, 26 Nov 2026

  2. Safaricom adds OTP verification before beneficiaries can be added to Shiriki Pay (opens in new tab)

    Sponsors now get a one-time password on their registered number, receive transaction notifications and can remove a beneficiary at any time.

    Why it matters: A real example of confirmation and notification controls on a shared-wallet payments feature.

    People Daily, 26 Nov 2026

  3. NCBA digital loan disbursements rise 27% to Sh1.29 trillion in nine months (opens in new tab)

    Fuliza, offered with Safaricom's M-Pesa, accounted for Sh1.1 trillion of Kenya disbursements; M-Shwari rose 5.45% to Sh75.4 billion.

    Why it matters: Shows the scale of mobile-money credit rails that Kenyan fintech engineering work plugs into.

    Business Daily, 26 Nov 2026
