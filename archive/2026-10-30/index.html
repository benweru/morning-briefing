AI and LLMs

  1. Grok Bot 0.78 adds shopping cards, Shop Pay and subscription templates (opens in new tab)

    Version 0.78.1 shows shopping results as product cards, adds a Buy now order you approve, Shop Pay in the Vault's Payments tab, and templates for free trials and unused subscriptions.

    Why it matters: xAI is pushing Grok Bot from answering into acting (buying, cancelling) with approval steps, the same agent-with-guardrails pattern he builds.

    The Tech Signal, 29 Oct 2026

  2. Google announces Gemini 4 Carbon, a cybersecurity-focused frontier model (opens in new tab)

    Google says Carbon ranks #1 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 at 59 and scores 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro; it starts with trusted cyber defenders in the Fairwind Program.

    Why it matters: Coding and secure-code gains at the frontier set the bar for the AI coding tools he uses every day.

    Google, 28 Oct 2026

  3. Mistral releases open weights for Large 4 (Le Chonk) (opens in new tab)

    The 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model is now open weight under a modified MIT license, priced at $0.70 input and $2.10 output per million tokens via the API.

    Why it matters: A large open-weight model he can self-host with vLLM or SGLang, relevant to his quantization and self-hosting experience.

    Mistral, 29 Oct 2026

Startups and funding

  1. OPay prices its NYSE IPO at $19, raising $304 million (opens in new tab)

    Nigeria's OPay sold 16 million ADSs at the low end of its $19–$21 range, giving it a market cap of about $4.0 billion; trading is expected to start on Oct. 30.

    Why it matters: The biggest African fintech listing in years, a test of how U.S. investors value African payments businesses like the ones he has worked in.

    IPOScoop, 29 Oct 2026

  2. Binance says $6.3bn in crypto was hit by a hack (opens in new tab)

    Binance says about $6.3bn of assets were impacted and about half frozen; early checks point to a compromised engineer's laptop, and TRM Labs attributes it to North Korea's TraderTraitor unit.

    Why it matters: Described as the largest hack in history, and it started with one engineer's machine, a reminder about access controls in fintech systems.

    BBC, 30 Oct 2026

  3. Wabi raises $55M at a $1.25B valuation for its personal AI agent (opens in new tab)

    Replika founder Eugenia Kuyda's Wabi, which builds mini apps as a personal agent, raised $55 million in a round led by IVP.

    Why it matters: More money flowing into consumer agent apps, the kind of startups hiring agent and full-stack engineers.

    TechCrunch, 29 Oct 2026

  4. Agent identity startup Mintive raises a $7M seed (opens in new tab)

    Mintive has raised $11 million total; its platform includes an MCP gateway, an LLM gateway and agent identity management, with more than 50 enterprise customers.

    Why it matters: MCP gateways and agent governance are becoming their own product category, close to his MCP and automation skills.

    TechCrunch, 29 Oct 2026
