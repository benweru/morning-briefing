Today in tech

10 stories from 14–15 October 2026, each with a note on why it matters for Ben.

AI and LLMs

  1. Meta releases open weights for Muse Spark Open (opens in new tab)

    An 816B-parameter (42B active) multimodal model with a 1M-token context, built for coding and agentic work; weights are on Hugging Face and it is served by Baseten, Fireworks AI, Groq, Modal, OpenRouter and others.

    Why it matters: A strong open-weight coding and agent model he can call through cheap API hosts for his own agent projects.

    Meta AI Research, 15 Oct 2026

  2. MBZUAI releases Jais 3, open Arabic agent models (opens in new tab)

    Six Apache 2.0 models from 0.9B to 375B parameters with a 512K-token context, trained to plan tasks, call tools and MCP servers and edit files while chatting in Arabic.

    Why it matters: Small open models (3.7B and 7B) that are tuned for tool calling are a useful template for running agents locally.

    Institute of Foundation Models, MBZUAI, 15 Oct 2026

Startups and funding

  1. Modal raises $750M Series D after passing $1B run rate (opens in new tab)

    Modal says usage tripled in five months and it grew more than 15x year over year; the round was led by Accel at a $15.75 billion valuation, per its announcement and coverage.

    Why it matters: Serverless AI infrastructure is booming, and Modal's sandboxes are what many coding agents run on.

    Modal, 14 Oct 2026

  2. Jio Platforms sets price band for India's biggest IPO (opens in new tab)

    Reliance's digital arm priced its IPO at ₹1,065-1,119 a share, raising about ₹30,213 crore at the top end; it opens October 21 and closes October 23, all fresh issue.

    Why it matters: A record tech IPO in an emerging market, a signal for how investors value telecom-plus-digital platforms like Safaricom.

    Mint, 14 Oct 2026

  3. Fragment raises $12M so AI agents can handle money safely (opens in new tab)

    The New York startup's double-entry ledger API lets agents automate billing and accounting while blocking invalid transactions; FirstMark and BoxGroup co-led the Series A.

    Why it matters: Agents plus fintech ledgers is exactly the overlap of his Kwara work, and a niche that is hiring.

    Fortune, 14 Oct 2026

  4. African startups raised $2.49B in nine months as Q3 rebounded (opens in new tab)

    315 disclosed deals so far in 2026, up 7.8% year on year; Q3 alone brought $1.025 billion, and Kenya ranks fourth with $297 million.

    Why it matters: More late-stage African money means more local engineering hiring, including at Kenyan startups like ARC Ride and SunCulture.

    TechCabal, 15 Oct 2026

    African Development Bank, 14 Oct 2026

Kenya fintech

  1. Kenya's draft payments bill could slow fintech fundraising (opens in new tab)
