Part three: News

Today in tech

10 stories from 2–3 November 2026, each with a note on why it matters for Ben.

AI and LLMs

  1. Mistral publishes open weights for Mistral Large 4 (opens in new tab)

    The 1 trillion-parameter multimodal model (52 billion active) is on Hugging Face and via API; Mistral says it is the top open model on Artificial Analysis' Coding Agent Index and scored 41% on Terminal-Bench 4.0.

    Why it matters: A strong open-weight coding model he can self-host is useful for agent builds and for fintech clients that want code and data kept in-house.

    IT Brief Asia, 3 Nov 2026

  2. Grok Bot hits 800,000 paid weekly users as Musk flags serving costs (opens in new tab)

    SpaceX said Grok Bot reached 800,000 paid weekly users in early October, double September; Musk wrote that "the cost of serving Grok Bot was unsustainably high relative to revenue."

    Why it matters: Agent products are scaling fast but are expensive to run, so engineers who can make agents efficient are in demand.

    Fortune, 3 Nov 2026

  3. Grok Bot preview build tests usage resets in exchange for training data (opens in new tab)

    A leak from prerelease build 1.1.0-pre.12 shows a "Get additional Grok Bot usage" prompt offering a reset if users allow their interactions to train SpaceXSI's models; it may not ship.

    Why it matters: Shows how agent companies are trying to balance usage limits, cost and data, which matters for anyone building on these tools.

    Tech City Authority, 3 Nov 2026

Startups and funding

  1. Fal raises $1.3B at a $16B valuation (opens in new tab)

    The AI inference startup's Series F was led by Bessemer with Nvidia, Sound Ventures and T. Rowe Price; it doubles April's $8 billion valuation, and Fal passed $800 million in annualized revenue.

    Why it matters: Inference platforms are among the fastest-growing AI employers and keep hiring engineers who integrate open models.

    TechCrunch, 3 Nov 2026

  2. Traversal raises $120M at a $1B valuation for AI site-reliability agents (opens in new tab)

    Kleiner Perkins, Redpoint and Khosla led the round; Traversal grew revenue more than nine-fold in a year and says it will accelerate engineering hiring.

    Why it matters: Another well-funded agentic-AI company growing its engineering team.

    SiliconANGLE, 3 Nov 2026

  3. 42AI comes out of stealth with $62.5M for a behavioral world model (opens in new tab)

    The Israeli startup says its model, which predicts how traders act, already runs at 10 large brokerages; the money came from founders and Infinity Group over three years.

    Why it matters: AI aimed at financial decision-making sits close to his fintech background.

    Value Add VC, 3 Nov 2026

Hiring market

  1. Dice: US tech postings up 20% year over year, AI/ML up 91% (opens in new tab)

    Tech job postings rose 4% month over month in October; AI and machine-learning postings grew 91% year over year, with AI Trainers and MLOps engineers among the fastest-growing titles.

    Why it matters: AI skills are where hiring is growing fastest, which is exactly where his CV is pointed.

    Dice, 3 Nov 2026

  2. Goldman Sachs: workers laid off by AI take about a month longer to find work (opens in new tab)

    Workers most likely displaced by AI in 2025 took roughly a month longer to find new jobs and were about 10 points more likely to still be unemployed a year later.

    Why it matters: A reminder that building AI skills, rather than competing with automation, is the safer path.

    Axios, 3 Nov 2026
