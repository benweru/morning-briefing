Part three: News

Today in tech

11 stories from 7–8 October 2026, each with a note on why it matters for Ben.

AI and LLMs

  1. JetBrains releases Mellum2.1 for coding agents (opens in new tab)

    12B MoE model with 2.5B active parameters under Apache 2.0; post-trained with reinforcement learning in real sandboxes so it can explore a repo, edit files and check its own changes.

    Why it matters: A fast, self-hostable coding-agent model he could try in local agent workflows.

    JetBrains blog, 8 Oct 2026

  2. Liquid AI releases open-weight d1 decision models (opens in new tab)

    d1-3B and d1-omni-600M are on Hugging Face; d1-3B answers in 8 ms on an RTX 4090 and produces an answer in a single forward pass instead of tokens.

    Why it matters: Small, fast models suit cheap on-device routing and classification steps in agent pipelines.

    Liquid AI blog, 7 Oct 2026

  3. Anthropic launches critical-infrastructure cyber program and free OSS Scanner (opens in new tab)

    Partners include Accenture, CrowdStrike and Palo Alto; OSS Scanner gives eligible open-source projects free periodic vulnerability scans from Anthropic’s strongest models.

    Why it matters: Shows how frontier models are being used for security tooling, adjacent to agent and automation work.

    SiliconANGLE, 8 Oct 2026

  4. LiteLLM open-sources Moyai, a self-hosted cloud coding agent (opens in new tab)

    Runs Claude Code or Codex on 100+ providers; LiteLLM says it would have cost about $21,700 against a $101,872 monthly Devin bill.

    Why it matters: A concrete, self-hostable example of background coding agents he could study or run.

    LiteLLM blog, 7 Oct 2026

Startups and funding

  1. YC-backed Bujeti launches Brain AI teammates for African finance teams (opens in new tab)

    Four agents (Fetch, Chaser, Watchdog, Concierge) are live for more than 2,000 finance users in Nigeria and Kenya; Bujeti is also building an MCP connector for Claude and ChatGPT.

    Why it matters: African fintech agent tooling with MCP plans sits next to his Kwara automation work.

    Disrupt Africa, 8 Oct 2026

  2. Kenyan fintech Cloud9 hits $1m funding with Alliance (opens in new tab)

    Alliance’s investment brings total funding to $1 million for cross-border payments, cards and multi-currency accounts; Cloud9 says more than 25,000 accounts have opened since early 2026.

    Why it matters: A Nairobi-facing payments startup raising money is a nearby hiring signal for fintech engineers.

    Fintech Global, 8 Oct 2026

  3. Nous Research raises $90M at a $1.5B valuation (opens in new tab)

    Series B led by Robot Ventures (Nvidia, USV, Menlo among backers); total funding $158 million; launching "Hermes for Businesses" agents.

    Why it matters: A well-funded open-source agent company moving into enterprise, likely hiring agent engineers.

    TechCrunch, 7 Oct 2026

Hiring market

  1. HubSpot cuts nearly 660 jobs, about 7% of staff (opens in new tab)

    8-K dated Oct 6 estimates $65–75 million in charges; CEO Yamini Rangan said the cut is not driven by AI efficiencies and aims for fewer management layers.

  2. Amazon confirms hundreds of retail job cuts (opens in new tab)

    Cuts mainly in Stores (customer service, selling-partner support and retail engineering); outlets report fewer than 1,000 white-collar roles, with staff in the US, India and the UK affected.

    GeekWire, 8 Oct 2026

Kenya fintech

  1. KRA to use eTIMS stock records against tax returns (opens in new tab)

    A September KRA notice requires TIMS/eTIMS users to keep purchase, sale, transfer and disposal stock records that the authority can compare with electronic invoices.

    Why it matters: Tighter electronic tax trails mean more integration and compliance tooling work for Kenyan fintechs.

    Techweez, 8 Oct 2026

  2. Equity's Finserve pilots Emma, a new mobile money wallet (opens in new tab)

    Pilot wallet on Google Play (100+ downloads) to send, receive, borrow, save and pay; sign-up in the app, via *765# or the Equitel SIM toolkit.

    Tech-ish, 7 Oct 2026
