AI and LLMs

  1. Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen for fast coding and agent work (opens in new tab)

    Rolling out across the Gemini API, AI Studio and the Gemini app; priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across a 1M-token context.

    Why it matters: A cheaper, fast model aimed at the agent workflows he builds.

    Google, 12 Nov 2026

  2. Salesforce open-sources SOV-Transcribe-1 speech model (opens in new tab)

    Open-weight ASR model with 4 billion active parameters (26 billion total), released on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0.

    Why it matters: A self-hostable transcription option for voice-agent and automation projects.

    Hugging Face blog, 12 Nov 2026

  3. Perplexity releases Decider v1.3 classification models (opens in new tab)

    Decider and Decider Flash v1.3 add exact text-span extraction and Apache-2.0 weights; Perplexity reports 91.6% mean accuracy across 63 query-classification tasks.

    Why it matters: Routing and classification models like this are building blocks for agent pipelines.

    Unite.AI, 12 Nov 2026
