Part three: News

Today in tech

9 stories from 17 November 2026, each with a note on why it matters for Ben.

AI and LLMs

  1. Vercel releases eve 1.0, an open-source framework for production agents (opens in new tab)

    On 17 November Vercel announced eve 1.0 as generally available after a June public preview, with 230+ releases since, approval controls for agent actions, and an open-source Workflow SDK that lets an agent resume from its last completed step after a crash.

    Why it matters: He builds agent and automation workflows, and durable execution plus approval controls are the production concerns he runs into.

    Vercel, 17 Nov 2026

  2. Microsoft launches Copilot SDK 2.0 for building agents (opens in new tab)

    On 17 November, at Ignite, Microsoft announced the Microsoft Copilot SDK (previously the GitHub Copilot SDK), with a runtime that hosts many isolated sessions in one process, available in TypeScript, Python, Go, .NET, Java and Rust.

    Why it matters: A TypeScript and Python SDK for building agents is directly usable with his stack.

    Microsoft Command Line, 17 Nov 2026

  3. Replit launches Missions, coordinated agent swarms for large goals (opens in new tab)

    On 17 November Replit said Missions lets a team define an outcome and budget while a swarm of agents plans, builds, tests and iterates; Replit says it built an in-house monitoring platform in 2 days within a $3,000 budget.

    Why it matters: It shows where agentic coding tools are heading, and Replit states the budget and boundaries are set by the user.

    Replit, 17 Nov 2026

  4. MBZUAI releases six fully open K2-Horizon models from 0.9B to 375B (opens in new tab)

    On 17 November AI Weekly reported that MBZUAI released K2-Horizon, six models from a 0.9B dense network to a 375B-A23B mixture-of-experts flagship, with intermediate checkpoints and data pipelines; the 375B and 36B models currently ship weights only.

    Why it matters: Small open-weight models in GGUF and MLX formats are easy to run locally for the kind of LLM prototyping on his CV.

    AI Weekly, 17 Nov 2026

    Disrupt Africa, 17 Nov 2026

  3. Nigeria-based Khaime raises $500,000 pre-seed for AI-agent commerce (opens in new tab)

    On 17 November Launch Base Africa listed Khaime among its deals: a $500,000 pre-seed led by Launch Africa Ventures for an agent-first commerce platform with an AI agent that handles chats and turns conversations into orders.

    Why it matters: A funded African startup built around AI agents and chat channels such as WhatsApp, close to his automation work.

    Launch Base Africa, 17 Nov 2026

Kenya fintech

  1. Payaza gets CBK authorisation to operate as a payment service provider in Kenya (opens in new tab)

    On 17 November Tuko reported that Payaza received Central Bank of Kenya authorisation as a payment service provider; the Nairametrics report says it covers gateway services such as card, mobile money and bank payments but not wallets or holding customer funds.

    Why it matters: A new licensed payment gateway entering Kenya adds competition and possibly integration work in the payments space he knows.

    Tuko, 17 Nov 2026

  2. Kenya's payments rules are under review as new providers enter (opens in new tab)

    On 17 November The Standard Business, via The Online Kenyan, reported that M-PESA processed 46.41 billion transactions worth Sh41.68 trillion in the year to March 2026 and that the draft National Payment System Bill, 2026 aims to promote interoperability, competition and consumer protection.

    Why it matters: The proposed bill could change licensing for the payment providers he may work for or integrate with.

    The Online Kenyan (The Standard), 17 Nov 2026
