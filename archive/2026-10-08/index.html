Part two: News

Today in tech

11 stories from 6–7 October 2026, each with a note on why it matters for Ben.

AI and LLMs

  1. Liquid AI releases open-weight d1 decision models (opens in new tab)

    d1-3B and d1-omni-600M are on Hugging Face; d1-3B answers in 8 ms on an RTX 4090 and produces an answer in a single forward pass instead of tokens.

    Why it matters: Small, fast models suit cheap on-device routing and classification steps in agent pipelines.

    Liquid AI blog, 7 Oct 2026

  2. Cohere Labs releases Tiny Aya L2-Thinker (opens in new tab)

    Open 3.35B model that reasons in the prompt language over 93% of the time across 60 languages; weights and reasoning data released.

    Why it matters: A small open model worth testing for Kiswahili-facing products.

    Cohere blog, 6 Oct 2026

  3. LiteLLM open-sources Moyai, a self-hosted cloud coding agent (opens in new tab)

    Runs Claude Code or Codex on 100+ providers; LiteLLM says it would have cost about $21,700 against a $101,872 monthly Devin bill.

    Why it matters: A concrete, self-hostable example of background coding agents he could study or run.

    LiteLLM blog, 7 Oct 2026

  4. AWS previews Strands Box, an open-source sandbox for AI agents (opens in new tab)

    Developer-preview local sandbox that pairs operating-system isolation with Dogwood semantic policy.

    Why it matters: Agent safety and sandboxing are becoming part of the agent-engineering job.

    Strands Agents blog (AWS), 7 Oct 2026

Startups and funding

  1. Nous Research raises $90M at a $1.5B valuation (opens in new tab)

    Series B led by Robot Ventures (Nvidia, USV, Menlo among backers); total funding $158 million; launching "Hermes for Businesses" agents.

    Why it matters: A well-funded open-source agent company moving into enterprise, likely hiring agent engineers.

    TechCrunch, 7 Oct 2026

  2. Siena raises $17M Series A for AI agents for consumer brands (opens in new tab)

    York IE led the round, bringing total funding to $29.7 million; building customer-service, shopping and social agents.

    Why it matters: Another agentic-AI startup with fresh money to hire.

    The Next Web, 6 Oct 2026

  3. FINCA Ventures grants go to six African startups, four of them Kenyan (opens in new tab)

    Grants of $40,000 to $100,000; Kenya's VunaPay got $100,000 for co-op payments and credit, and nesti $60,000 for rent-based credit histories.

    Why it matters: Kenyan fintechs building payment and credit infrastructure close to his Kwara work.

    Launch Base Africa, 7 Oct 2026

Hiring market

  1. Stanford study: AI-exposed jobs for 22-25-year-olds 19% below trend (opens in new tab)

    In jobs like customer service and software engineering, employment for ages 22-25 is 19% lower than if it had kept pace since 2022; no gap for experienced workers.

    Why it matters: Junior roles are scarcer where AI automates tasks, so showing AI-building skills matters.

    Fast Company, 7 Oct 2026

  2. Amazon makes fresh job cuts, mainly in its retail unit (opens in new tab)

    Fewer than 1,000 white-collar jobs cut, according to a person familiar, after an earlier round of 30,000; staff in the US, India and the UK affected.

    Why it matters: Big-tech cuts keep adding experienced people to the remote job pool.

    Reuters (via KRRO), 7 Oct 2026

Kenya fintech

  1. Equity's Finserve pilots Emma, a new mobile money wallet (opens in new tab)

    Pilot wallet on Google Play (100+ downloads) to send, receive, borrow, save and pay; sign-up in the app, via *765# or the Equitel SIM toolkit.

    Why it matters: A new M-PESA rival means more Kenyan wallet and payments integration work.

    Tech-ish, 7 Oct 2026

  2. Safaricom's Jumbe SMS proves M-PESA payments without showing your balance (opens in new tab)

    "Split Notifications" send a second SMS with the transaction code, amount, business, Till and time, plus a JumbeID instead of name and number.

    Why it matters: Shows how M-PESA's merchant APIs and notifications are changing.

    Techweez, 6 Oct 2026
