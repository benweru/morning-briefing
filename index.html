Today in tech

9 stories from 19–20 October 2026, each with a note on why it matters for Ben.

AI and LLMs

  1. SpaceX's AI arm, home of Grok and Cursor, beats expectations (opens in new tab)

    SpaceX reported $14.6 billion in Q3 revenue; its "Super Intelligence" unit (Cursor, Grok bot and its neocloud with 1.7 GW of compute) brought in $7.5 billion and $431 million in profit, and AI is now 52% of income.

    Why it matters: xAI/Grok is now a profitable business line, so Grok and Cursor tooling skills are worth showing on his CV.

    TechCrunch, 20 Oct 2026

  2. Grok Bot can now answer X DMs and be the Android assistant (opens in new tab)

    A "What's new in Grok Bot" video lists 12 changes, including reading and replying to X Chat DMs, acting when tagged @bot on X, Android default-assistant support and 30 minutes of voice-call memory.

    Why it matters: Agent features he uses daily are shipping weekly, which is good material for agent-focused applications.

    Tesla North, 20 Oct 2026

  3. Inception's Mercury 3 diffusion model claims 4-8x the speed of GPT-6 Luna (opens in new tab)

    Mercury 3 ran at 1,068 tokens per second, beat GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 on 8 of 12 tests in Inception's own benchmarks, has a 1M-token context and lists at $0.20/$0.70 per million tokens.

    Why it matters: A fast, OpenAI-compatible model he could try for low-latency agent or chat features.

    SiliconANGLE, 20 Oct 2026

  4. Meta's open-weight Muse Spark Open goes live on Fireworks (opens in new tab)

    An 816B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 42B active parameters and a 1M-token context, ranked first of 75 models on Vals AI's legal agent leaderboard at 25.4%.

    Why it matters: A strong open-weights option for finance-style agent work, close to his fintech background.

    Fireworks AI, 20 Oct 2026

Startups and funding

  1. Salesforce agrees to buy Braze for about $5.66 billion (opens in new tab)

    Braze Class A holders get $47.50 a share in cash; the deal is expected to close in Salesforce's fiscal 2028, subject to shareholder and regulatory approval.

    Why it matters: Big-company buying of AI-driven customer-engagement tools keeps demand up for integration and automation engineers.

    Salesforce, 20 Oct 2026

  2. Global fintech funding fell 21% in Q3 as deal count hit a low (opens in new tab)

    Fintech startups raised $10.6 billion in Q3 2026, down 21.4% year over year, across just 687 rounds, the fewest since at least Q1 2018.

    Why it matters: Fewer, larger fintech rounds mean fewer junior fintech openings; investors favour companies with real AI products.

    Crunchbase News, 20 Oct 2026

Kenya fintech

  1. Nedbank completes its takeover of 66% of NCBA (opens in new tab)

    The deal is worth about 13.9 billion rand (about $800 million); NCBA stays listed in Nairobi with the same brand and leadership, and its shares are suspended from about Oct 21 to Nov 2.

    Why it matters: A major Kenyan bank now has South African backing, which could mean new digital-banking projects and tech hiring.

    TechMoran, 19 Oct 2026

  2. Mastercard and Moovn sign a five-year East Africa payments deal (opens in new tab)

    MoovnPay will add prepaid Mastercard cards, tokenization, Mastercard Payment Gateway Services and cross-border transfers across Kenya and Tanzania.

    Why it matters: Another Nairobi payments platform scaling up wallets and merchant acceptance, the kind of integration work he did at Kwara.

    Mastercard, 19 Oct 2026

  3. Kenya's draft payments bill would give the CBK sweeping powers (opens in new tab)

    The draft National Payment System Bill, 2026 creates about 10 licence categories with minimum capital from roughly $39,000 to nearly $2 million for e-money issuers, and lets the CBK install statutory managers for up to 12 months.

    Why it matters: Will shape which Kenyan fintechs can launch and hire, and is useful context for fintech interviews.

    This Week in Fintech, 19 Oct 2026
