Today in tech

8 stories from 8–9 November 2026, each with a note on why it matters for Ben.

AI and LLMs

  1. Google adds TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max for secure and long-context agents (opens in new tab)

    On Nov 9 Google expanded its eighth-generation TPU line with TPU 8tx (confidential inference / small-scale training) and TPU 8i Max (dual-chip package for large context windows and higher rack density), the first time it has shipped four TPU systems in one year.

    Why it matters: Agentic and long-context hardware is what the stacks he builds on will run against, and Google also called out TorchTPU plus Cursor/Claude Code helpers for TPU onboarding.

    Google Cloud Blog, 9 Nov 2026

  2. Grok Bot can now call US businesses for paying subscribers (opens in new tab)

    PYMNTS reports Grok Bot dials from a temporary number, works phone menus, waits on hold with a live transcript, negotiates bills and books appointments; users stay legally responsible for each call, and long holds burn the weekly usage allowance.

    Why it matters: Shows how far consumer agents have moved into multi-step real-world tasks—the same pattern as the agent workflows on his CV.

    PYMNTS, 9 Nov 2026

  3. Musk says xAI is cutting Grok Bot token use and tightening behaviour (opens in new tab)

    In a Nov 8 X post covered Nov 9, Musk said xAI is working on more efficient token use and smarter behaviour for Grok Bot, which already has email, calendar and scheduling features for multi-step requests.

    Why it matters: Usage limits and cost control on the agent he uses daily will shape how far he can lean on it for applications and take-homes.

    TokenPost, 9 Nov 2026

  4. Anthropic reportedly locks in $3.4B of gas generators for data-centre power (opens in new tab)

    The Financial Times story, surfaced Nov 8–9, says Anthropic took over about $3.4 billion of gas-generator orders (about 1.7 GW) from Texas-based OnePWR Solutions to secure power for future AI data centres; neither side had publicly confirmed when Reuters covered it.

    Why it matters: Power, not just models, is now the bottleneck for the labs that hire AI engineers and evaluators.

    The Neuron (FT / Reuters), 9 Nov 2026

Startups and funding

  1. WayaWaya and Choice Bank launch AI-adaptive banking in Kenya (opens in new tab)

    On Nov 9 WayaWaya launched WayaWaya Banking with Choice Bank under CBK rules: Choice supplies the banking rails while WayaWaya’s AI personalises dashboards for business, salary and investor users, with a longer-term pan-African neobank plan.

    Why it matters: A Nairobi fintech shipping AI on top of banking APIs is close to the Kwara-style work on his CV.

    TechAfrica News, 9 Nov 2026

Hiring market

  1. Only 1 in 4 new tech jobs was remote last week (opens in new tab)

    Siftwright’s Nov 2–8 ATS sweep of ~7,300 company boards found 3,247 new software/data/AI roles, of which 25% (805) were remote; junior/intern/graduate titles were about 5% (153), and 28% of tech postings mentioned AI/ML/LLM/agents.

    Why it matters: Confirms why Kenya-open remote shortlists stay thin and why he should move fast on the few that fit.

    DEV Community, 9 Nov 2026

Kenya fintech

  1. Safaricom’s Fintech 2.0 lifts M-PESA to about 6,000 TPS (opens in new tab)

    In coverage dated Nov 9 of the FY2026 annual report, Safaricom says Fintech 2.0 rebuilt M-PESA as a microservices hybrid-cloud platform, raising capacity from about 4,500 to about 6,000 uninterrupted transactions per second with headroom to 10,000, plus AI security and open APIs.

    Why it matters: More open M-PESA APIs and higher capacity mean more fintech integration work of the kind he already ships.

    Tuko.co.ke, 9 Nov 2026

  2. Safaricom says it has trained 6,000 staff in AI and ML (opens in new tab)

    On Nov 8 TechMoran reported Safaricom’s PACE programme has given more than 6,000 employees foundational to associate AI/ML skills, with CPO Florence Nyokabi writing that the company aims to automate tasks, not people, as it shifts toward a tech company.

    Why it matters: Signals ongoing AI product and automation hiring demand inside Kenya’s largest tech employer ecosystem.

    TechMoran, 8 Nov 2026
