11 stories from 31 October – 1 November 2026, each with a note on why it matters for Ben.

AI and LLMs

  1. xAI will watermark Grok API text by default from November 7 (opens in new tab)

    Output from seven Grok API models, including grok-4.7 and grok-4.6, will carry an invisible statistical watermark with an x-watermark response header, to comply with the EU AI Act.

    Why it matters: If you build on the Grok API, nothing breaks, but generated text becomes detectable; worth knowing for any LLM product work.

    GPTOnline, 1 Nov 2026

  2. OpenAI folds Work features into ChatGPT and merges ChatGPT and Codex limits (opens in new tab)

    OpenAI will test a combined Chat and Work experience with a small group, and ChatGPT and Codex will draw from one shared allowance; Instant chats won't count.

    Why it matters: Agentic features are moving into the main chat product, which is the kind of tooling AI automation roles now expect you to know.

    BleepingComputer, 1 Nov 2026

  3. Judge lets NAACP Clean Air Act suit against xAI's Colossus data center proceed (opens in new tab)

    A federal judge denied xAI's motion to dismiss a suit claiming it ran at least 35 combustion turbines at the Colossus site without required permits.

    Why it matters: Shows the permitting and regulatory risk behind big AI compute build-outs.

    The Morning Build, 1 Nov 2026
