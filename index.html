Today in tech

9 stories from 24–25 November 2026, each with a note on why it matters for Ben.

AI and LLMs

  1. Ant Group releases Ling-3.2-air, an MIT-licensed multimodal model for tool-using agents (opens in new tab)

    Weights are out under the MIT licence: 124 billion parameters in total with 5.5 billion activated per token, with SGLang and vLLM deployment instructions. Ant reports AutomationBench-AA task completion rising from 15.7% to 61%.

    Why it matters: An open-weights model aimed at agents that finish office and browser tasks with tools, which is the kind of workflow he builds in n8n.

    TechNode, 25 Nov 2026

  2. Amazon Transcribe Turbo enters public preview with turn-taking for voice agents (opens in new tab)

    Streaming word error rate drops about 14% relative in English; semantic turn-taking responds in about 615 ms with a 5% interruption rate; runtime ASR hints raise English menu-routing accuracy from 87% to 97%. The preview is in US East (Ohio).

    Why it matters: Voice-agent plumbing (turn detection, per-turn hints) is something a webhook-and-API engineer could wire into customer-support flows.

    AWS, 25 Nov 2026

  3. Amini releases Soma-1X Lite, a 2B open-weight model for document intelligence (opens in new tab)

    It scores 84.7 on olmOCR-Bench, runs about 15,000 pages an hour on one GPU at an estimated $0.22 per 1,000 pages, and had the lowest average error rate of five open models on ten African languages including Swahili.

    Why it matters: An African team shipping a small open model that reads Swahili documents, relevant to document-ingestion pipelines like his Kwara loan one.

    Amini AI, 25 Nov 2026

Startups and funding

  1. Maple raises $5.3M seed for private AI chat and a desktop agent (opens in new tab)

    Epic Ventures led the round; models run inside trusted execution environments so Maple cannot read user data, and the team of four says it is actively hiring.

    Why it matters: A small agentic-AI company that is hiring, with an open-source trust model he could read and learn from.

    TechCrunch, 24 Nov 2026

  2. Permut raises EUR 3M seed for conversational AI agents that handle support and sales (opens in new tab)

    The agents handle 80-85% of support enquiries without a human and run on web, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and email; the team will grow from nine to 20 people.

    Why it matters: Agent integrations across channels with internal-system actions are close to his webhook and automation work.

    Tech.eu, 25 Nov 2026

Hiring market

  1. Skyscanner plans to cut 150 jobs, about 10% of staff, as AI changes travel search (opens in new tab)

    Software development and middle-management roles are among those reportedly affected, alongside marketing, commercial, workplace and strategy teams.

    Why it matters: Another sign that AI-driven restructuring is hitting software teams, so a visible portfolio of shipped work matters.

    Travel Weekly, 24 Nov 2026

  2. Pipedrive notifies Estonia of 236 layoffs and plans to close Lisbon and Prague offices (opens in new tab)

    The cuts equal 62% of its 380 staff in Estonia, out of nearly 800 employees in total; developers in Berlin are also affected.

    Why it matters: A reminder that European product companies are consolidating, which can shrink hiring there.

    WeekCRM, 25 Nov 2026

Kenya fintech

  1. Vodafone Group plans data centres in Kenya through Vodafone Business (opens in new tab)

    Kenya and Egypt are the next countries after a 4 MW facility opened in Izmir, Turkey, in September; Vodafone has not said whether Vodacom, Safaricom's parent, is a partner.

    Why it matters: More local compute for cloud and AI workloads, and a possible source of Nairobi infrastructure roles.

    Business Daily, 25 Nov 2026

  2. Safaricom restores M-PESA GlobalPay virtual card after a two-day technical fault (opens in new tab)

    Safaricom said on 23 November that a technical issue affected the virtual Visa card; on 25 November M-PESA said the service is back, with no cause given.

    Why it matters: A real example of a payments outage on a wallet-funded card, useful when thinking about retries and status messaging.

    People Daily, 25 Nov 2026
