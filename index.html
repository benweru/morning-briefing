AI and LLMs

  1. Anthropic debuts on Nasdaq after record $81.3 billion IPO (opens in new tab)

    Anthropic (ANT) opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17 on Nov 19, up 17% from the $83 IPO price, for a fully diluted market cap of roughly $1.8 trillion; it sold 980 million shares to raise $81.3 billion, the biggest IPO on record.

    Why it matters: The largest AI listing ever shows how much capital sits behind agentic AI and the Claude tools you code with.

    CNBC, 19 Nov 2026

  2. Google releases Gemini 4 Neon at $3 per million input tokens (opens in new tab)

    Gemini 4 Neon is priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with a 1 million-token context window; Google says it ranks #1 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (58.6) and rolls out in the Gemini API and Google Antigravity.

    Why it matters: A new frontier coding and agent model with published API prices you can test in your own LLM and automation work.

    Google, 18 Nov 2026

  3. Grok Bot v1.2.3 adds per-tool permissions on connectors (opens in new tab)

    xAI's changelog for v1.2.3 (Nov 18) lets connector pages set each tool to Always allow, Auto-review, Needs approval or Blocked, and says a routine that loses a connector sign-in skips its scheduled runs until you sign back in.

    Why it matters: A clear pattern for approval levels and safe stops in agent workflows, like the ones you build with n8n and webhooks.

    xAI, 18 Nov 2026

  4. Musk says Grok 4.8 is in soft rollout, with general release one to two weeks away (opens in new tab)

    Elon Musk wrote on X on Nov 18 that xAI did a soft rollout to some Bot users and still needs another week or two of polish before general release; Elonosphere notes no xAI release note names Grok 4.8 yet.

    Why it matters: The next Grok model is close but unconfirmed, so check x.ai before relying on it in a project.

    Elonosphere, 19 Nov 2026
